## 🏛️ PRD Structure & Scope

The PRD covers:
1. **Executive Summary & Governance**: Scope, evidence standards, verification tiers, and operational reality.
2. **Problem Framing & Market Analysis**: Social commerce friction in India, market size ($13.8B - $14B bakery), and discovery plans.
3. **User Segments & Proto-Personas**: Celebration Planners (Demand) & Artisan Bakery Merchants (Supply).
4. **Product Vision & Metrics**: Deterministic pricing integrity, candidate North Star metrics, and strategic prioritization.
5. **End-to-End User Journeys**: Customer 8-step builder flow, bakery order management, and operations console.
6. **Scope Boundaries**: MVP vs Phase 2-4 roadmaps and explicit non-goals.
7. **Prioritized Requirements & Acceptance Criteria**: Functional specs across custom studio, cart, checkout, and tracking.
8. **Order State Machine & Exceptions**: 8-state progression, single-bakery cart isolation, and mandatory rejection logging.
9. **Non-Functional Requirements & Security**: Dietary assurance, allergen cross-contamination policies, and data security.
10. **Trade-offs & Open Product Decisions**: Payment capture models, 3PL logistics vs self-delivery, and take-rate dynamics.
